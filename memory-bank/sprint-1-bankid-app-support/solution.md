# Solution: Use BankID's documented app support paths

When adding BankID-facing support copy or troubleshooting, map users to the
documented BankID actions.

## Support guidance

- Tell users they get BankID through their bank.
- Tell users to activate the app with a code unit when other activation methods
  fail.
- If push notifications do not work, tell users to enable notifications and
  check focus or do-not-disturb settings.
- If activation reports incorrect contact information, tell users to update or
  reinstall the app, try another activation method, or verify that their bank
  has a confirmed phone number.
- If the app is locked after failed attempts, tell users to contact their bank.
- If the user changes phone, they must reactivate the app on the new device.

## Product cautions

- The BankID app can only be active on one device at a time.
- BankID with biometrics depends on the site offering that assurance flow.
- Users still need their BankID password for high-risk operations.
- Biometric data stays on the user's device according to the source notes.
