# BankID with code unit or app

With BankID you identify yourself online by using your Norwegian national identity number together with a one-time password code unit or an app.

Your BankID works in all Norwegian banks and most public services. Additionally, you can use BankID for electronic signing and identification on a wide range of services.

## Don't have BankID?

You can get BankID from your bank. In many banks, you can get BankID when you are 12 years old. To get BankID, you need to verify your identity. Visit your bank's website for assistance in getting started with BankID.

Banks offer different ways to generate one-time codes, including code units and apps. Your bank will provide you with what you need to get started with BankID.
